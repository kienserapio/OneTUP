## Section 3 — What it does

**Heading**
Six things you check every day

**Intro**
Import your schedule once. Everything else builds on it.

---

**Your next class, and when to leave for it**

OneTUP works backwards from your first class — how long the commute takes, whether it's rush hour, whether it's raining, how long you take to get ready — and tells you when to wake up and when to walk out the door.

---

**Cuts, counted for you**

One tap after class. OneTUP does the arithmetic and warns you before you hit the limit, per subject, so you find out with two absences left instead of none.

---

**GWA, and what you'd need**

See where you stand now. Set a target and see the grade each remaining subject needs to get you there. Get warned when a scholarship or Dean's List threshold starts slipping.

---

**Every deadline in one list**

Snap a photo of the whiteboard, or share the message from your class GC. It becomes a deadline with a reminder attached. Everything due, across every subject, sorted by what's closest.

---

**Announcements that actually reach you**

Class suspended, quiz moved, room changed. Your class representative posts it once and it reaches everyone in the section, tagged to the right subject, with an option to turn it into a deadline in one tap.

---

**How to get to TUP, with real fares**

Routes from your area with the student discount already applied, actual travel times, and a warning when it's rush hour. The routes come from students who ride them every day, not from a map that doesn't know jeepneys exist.

## Section 4 — The commute

**Heading**
Ask how to get to TUP. See it on a map.

**Body**
Google Maps doesn't know jeepney routes, doesn't know fares, and has never heard of the 20% student discount. OneTUP does, because the routes come from students.

**Example question 1**
"How do I get to TUP from Caloocan?"

**Example answer 1**
Three ways work from Grace Park. The fastest is a jeep to Monumento, LRT-1 to Central Terminal, then an eight-minute walk — about 55 minutes for ₱41 with your student discount.

**Example question 2**
"What's the cheapest way home tonight?"

**Example answer 2**
Bus from Lawton via EDSA is ₱26 but takes 75 minutes. The last northbound LRT-1 trip leaves Central Terminal at 9:30 PM, so decide before then.

## Section 5 — Wake-up and leave-by

**Heading**
The alarm that knows about traffic

**Body**
Set your first class and where you live. OneTUP handles the rest: it counts backwards through the commute, adds time for rush hour on your actual route, adds more if rain is forecast, leaves room for you to get ready, and sets the alarm.

When something changes — a class moved, a route got slower — the alarm moves with it, and tells you why.

**Example**
Leave by 5:40 AM. Wake at 4:55.
*Rush hour adds about 20 minutes on LRT-1 and rain is forecast at 6 AM, so your alarm moved 15 minutes earlier.*

## Section 8 — How it works

**Heading**
Three steps, about two minutes

**Step 1 — Bring in your schedule**
Connect your ERS once and your schedule imports itself. Your password is used to read the page and then discarded — OneTUP never saves it. If you'd rather not, paste your schedule instead. It works the same.

**Step 2 — Tell it where you live**
Pick your area and your usual route so OneTUP can work out your leave-by and wake-up times.

**Step 3 — Add it to your home screen**
That's what turns on reminders and lets your schedule load without signal.

## Section 9 — Trust

**Eyebrow**
Your data

**Heading**
What OneTUP does and doesn't do

**Point 1 — Never saves your password**
Not your ERS login, not your Facebook. Your ERS password is used once to read your schedule, then discarded. There's no place in OneTUP where it's stored.

**Point 2 — Grades and cuts stay yours**
Only you can see them. No section rankings, no aggregate reports, nothing visible to faculty or admin. Not now and not later — it's built so that isn't possible.

**Point 3 — AI is labelled where it's used**
It drafts reviewers from your notes and helps phrase your own words. It never invents your grades, your prerequisites, or a jeepney route. Those come from your data or from real sources, with the source shown.

**Point 4 — You can take it all with you**
Export everything, any time. Delete your account and it's actually deleted.

### Consent

**Heading**
About your ERS password

**Body**
To import your schedule, OneTUP signs in to ERS as you, once, and reads your schedule page. Here's exactly what happens:

- Your student number, ERS password, and birthdate are sent over an encrypted connection.
- They're used to sign in, read your schedule, and then discarded.
- Your schedule is saved. Your password is not — there's nowhere in OneTUP that stores it.
- Nothing in your ERS account is changed, submitted, or read beyond the schedule page.

Being straight with you: your password does pass through our server while this happens, and exists in memory for a few seconds. We don't keep it, but it isn't zero risk.

If you'd rather not, paste your schedule instead. Everything else works the same.

**Actions**
Connect ERS · Paste it instead
